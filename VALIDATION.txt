  by these acceptance checks. The LCD brightness/configuration was exercised.

RELEASE LIMIT
This is a substantial experimental implementation for the identified devices,
not a verified universal or feature-for-feature replacement for all TT RGB Plus
products. Keep the existing proven cooling setup until controlled hardware
acceptance tests have passed. Do not delete TT RGB Plus yet.
